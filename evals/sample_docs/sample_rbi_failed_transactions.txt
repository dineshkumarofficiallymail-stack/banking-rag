1. Purpose

This sample circular harmonises the turn around time (TAT) for failed transactions and the compensation payable to customers across sample payment systems.

2. Failed Transactions at ATMs

2.1 Cash Not Dispensed
Where a customer's account is debited but cash is not dispensed in this sample scenario, the issuing bank must reverse the transaction within T+5 working days. Beyond T+5, a sample compensation of Rs 100 per day of delay is payable to the customer without the customer having to raise a complaint.

3. Failed IMPS and UPI Transactions

3.1 Beneficiary Not Credited
In the sample procedure, if the remitter's account is debited but the beneficiary's account is not credited, the remitter bank must auto-reverse the amount within T+1 working day. Compensation of Rs 100 per day applies if the reversal happens after T+1.

4. Card Transactions

4.1 Point of Sale
For sample card-present transactions where the account is debited but confirmation is not received at the merchant, auto-reversal must be completed within T+5 working days with the same Rs 100 per day compensation beyond that.
